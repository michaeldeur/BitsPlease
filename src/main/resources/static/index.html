Three factors. One secure login.

FaceAuth protects every account with a password, a code from your authenticator app, and AI facial recognition through your webcam.

Get started I already have an account
🔑

Something you know

Your password, stored only as a salted BCrypt hash in the SQL database.

📱

Something you have

A 6-digit code from Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy (TOTP, RFC 6238).

🙂

Something you are

A webcam face scan. A pre-trained neural network turns your face into 128 numbers that are matched on the server.
